# Wise plc — Company Research

## Company Overview

### Company
Wise plc

### Industry
FinTech / International Payments

### What does Wise do?
Wise is a global technology and FinTech company focused on international money movement and financial services.

It allows individuals and businesses to send, receive, hold and spend money internationally across multiple currencies.

Its products include Wise Account, Wise Business and Wise Platform, which provides Wise's infrastructure to financial institutions and other organisations.

In FY2025, Wise supported around 15.6 million people and businesses and processed more than £145 billion of cross-border transactions

### Main Products and Services
Wise Account:
For individuals who need to manage money internationally, including holding currencies, sending money and spending abroad.

Wise Business:
For businesses that need to make and receive international payments and manage multiple currencies.

Wise Platform:
Provides Wise's infrastructure to banks, fintechs and
large enterprises.

### Customers
Wise serves both personal and business customers.

Personal customers include people such as travellers, expats and freelancers.

Business customers include SMEs and larger organisations that need international payment capabilities.

### Geographic Markets
Wise operates internationally and supports transfers across more than 40 currencies and more than 160 countries.

Its major geographic markets include the UK, Europe,
North America, Asia-Pacific and other international markets.

### Business Model — How Wise Makes Money
Wise primarily generates revenue from fees charged on cross-border transactions.

Additional revenue comes from:
- interest income on customer balances
- debit card interchange fees
- other Wise Account features
- services provided through Wise Platform

### Main Competitors
Wise operates in a competitive international payments and financial services market.

Key competitors include:

- PayPal — digital payments and international money transfers.
- Western Union — established international money transfer provider with a large global network.
- Revolut — digital financial services platform offering international payments and multi-currency accounts.
- Remitly — digital provider focused on international remittances.
- Traditional banks — compete with Wise through their international transfer and foreign exchange services.

Competition can affect Wise through pricing, customer
acquisition, transaction volumes and product development.

### Growth Drivers
Growth Drivers could include customer growth, increased transaction volume. Aslo expansion into new markets and new currencies means Wise Business and Platform growth as there are more customers.
Potential growth drivers include:
- Customer growth, which can increase the number of transactions and overall transaction volume.
- Increased transaction volume from existing customers, which can increase fee revenue.
- Expansion into new geographic markets and currencies, increasing Wise's addressable customer base and potential transaction volume.
- Growth of Wise Business and Wise Platform, which could increase the number and value of transactions processed through the business.

### Key Risks
Potential risks include:

- Competition from other payment and financial-services companies, which could put pressure on Wise's pricing and transaction fees.
- Regulatory requirements across the countries and      markets in which Wise operates, which could increase compliance costs and operational complexity.
- Changes in interest rates, which could affect interest income earned on customer balances.
- Operating costs increasing faster than revenue, which could put pressure on profitability.
- Slower customer growth or lower transaction volumes, which could reduce revenue growth.
