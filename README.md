# Wise plc — Investment Research

## Overview

A financial analysis and valuation project on **Wise plc (LSE: WISE)**.

The project analyses Wise's historical financial performance, forecasts future results, and estimates the company's value using multiple valuation methods.

> **Note:** This is an educational project and is not financial advice.

## What I Analysed

- Wise's business model and financial performance
- Historical revenue and profitability
- Revenue and earnings growth
- Key financial ratios
- Future financial performance
- Business and financial risks

## Financial Forecast

I built a forecast for **2027–2029** using assumptions for:

- Revenue growth
- Operating margins
- Tax
- Capital expenditure
- Working capital
- Other operating costs

The assumptions were based on historical performance, company guidance, and reasonable financial modelling assumptions.

## Valuation Methods

Three valuation approaches were used:

### P/E Valuation

Values the company by applying a P/E multiple to forecast earnings.

```text
Equity Value = Forecast Net Income × P/E Multiple
```

### EV/Revenue

Values the company using a multiple of forecast revenue.

```text
Enterprise Value = Forecast Revenue × EV/Revenue Multiple
```

### DCF

Estimates the value of Wise based on the present value of its expected future cash flows.

```text
Forecast Cash Flows
        ↓
Discount Future Cash Flows
        ↓
Terminal Value
        ↓
Enterprise Value
        ↓
Equity Value
```

## Key Takeaways

The analysis showed:

- Strong growth in customers and transaction volumes
- Continued focus on low-cost international transfers
- Potential operating leverage as the business scales
- Increasing importance of non-transfer revenue
- Valuation is highly sensitive to growth and profitability assumptions
